What it does

One operating system for delivery visibility.

Rider Logistics connects customer booking, driver availability, route-matched dispatch, live driver tracking, and admin supervision in one production-ready workflow.

01

Customers

See nearby available drivers before booking, track the assigned driver after acceptance, and follow every movement from pickup to drop-off.

02

Drivers

Go Online or Offline, broadcast live location only while online, receive requests, and accept single or route-matched delivery jobs.

03

Admins

Monitor online drivers on a live map, filter by status and vehicle type, and flag risky behavior during active deliveries.

Live tracking

Know who is available, where they are, and what happens next.

The platform updates driver status in real time. Online drivers can appear to customers, receive delivery requests, and move between AVAILABLE, BUSY, and ON_DELIVERY states. Offline drivers are hidden from customer discovery while admins retain last known location and last active time.

  • Nearby driver discovery before checkout
  • Automatic customer tracking after assignment
  • ETA from acceptance to pickup and drop-off
  • Driver rating, vehicle type, and completed-job profile

Fraud and safety rules

Built to protect packages, customers, and trusted drivers.

Offline during delivery

If a driver goes offline while assigned to an active delivery, Rider Logistics creates an alert for admin review.

Location disabled with package

If location sharing is disabled while a package is in hand, the platform raises a safety warning and increases risk visibility.

Contact harvesting pattern

If a driver repeatedly goes online, collects customer contact, then cancels, the fraud score increases for investigation.
